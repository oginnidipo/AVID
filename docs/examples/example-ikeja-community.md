## Where this stands

Ikeja Community School had no dedicated library space at all. The school
offered a disused storeroom next to the head teacher's office — 4.2 by 5.8
metres, concrete floor, no ceiling and no electrical supply.

Work began in October. The roof and ceiling are complete and the room is now
watertight. Electrical work is scheduled for the second week of November, and
shelving is being built in Mushin by the same carpenter who supplied St Mary's.

Book selection is under way with the school's head teacher. We will update this
record as each stage completes, including if it slips.
